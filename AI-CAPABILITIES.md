## What to conclude — and what not to conclude

The strongest claim supported by source + real acceptance is **an integrated, bounded local AI pipeline with durable asynchronous processing, SQL grant-first cross-modal retrieval and extractive citation answers**, plus separately tested AI research/examples. It is a useful **Tech Lead / Solution Architect** case study because designs, invariants, limits and negative tests are readable without running models.

A code-review agent should cite **file/function/test**, distinguish **integrated vs separate vs prototype**, and avoid upgrading an aspirational architecture diagram to deployed functionality.
